## 1. How Fluxo Stores and Syncs Your Content

Fluxo is a **note-taking and learning application**. It keeps a working copy of your content on your device so the app stays fast and usable offline, and it **synchronizes that content with the Fluxo backend**.

When you are signed in, the content you create in Fluxo, including:

* notes and their text, formatting, and structure;
* spaces and folders;
* images, photos, and files you attach to or import into notes;
* study and learning statistics, such as review history, practice results, activity days, and streaks;
* summaries, quizzes, flashcards, and other study materials generated for your notes; and
* your app settings and preferences,

is sent to and stored on our backend as soon as a connection is available.

We store this content on our servers so that we can:

* give you access to the same content across your devices;
* keep a server-side copy that can restore your content if you lose, replace, or reset a device; and
* provide features that require server processing, such as AI features and file storage.

Changes you make while offline are stored on your device and synchronized when your device reconnects.

We store the content you create or add to Fluxo, including:

## 3. AI Features and Third-Party AI Providers

Fluxo offers AI features that turn your notes into study materials and help you organize them, including:

* summaries;
* quizzes;
* flashcards and other practice materials; and
* suggestions for the space or folder a note belongs in.

### 3.1 What we send

When you use an AI feature, or when a feature that relies on AI runs for a note, our backend sends the information needed for that request to a third-party AI provider through its API. This may include:

* the text of the relevant note or notes;
* note titles and the names of your spaces and folders;
* the names of files attached to the note;
* the language of your content; and
* instructions that describe the requested result.

Images and photos, the contents of attached files, your email address, and your payment information are not included in AI requests. Text from an imported document is included only after it has become part of a note.

### 3.2 Which providers we use

We currently use the following AI providers:

* **OpenAI** — our primary provider for generating summaries, quizzes, flashcards, and other study materials;
* **Anthropic** — used as a fallback provider when the primary provider is unavailable; and
* **TypeSafe AI** — used to classify notes, for example to suggest the space a note belongs in.

We may change or add AI providers over time. When we do, we will update this Privacy Policy.

### 3.3 How the information is used

Your content is shared with these providers **only to provide the AI features of the Fluxo application** that you use.

We do not sell your content, and we do not share it with AI providers for advertising.

We use these providers through their business APIs. Their handling of the data they receive is governed by their own API terms and privacy policies. OpenAI and Anthropic state in their published API policies that, by default, they do not use data submitted through their APIs to train their models. TypeSafe AI states in its privacy policy that it does not train or fine-tune models on the input it receives. These providers may keep request data for a limited time under their own policies, for example for abuse monitoring.

### 3.4 AI request records

To operate AI features, account for AI credits, investigate errors, monitor costs, and prevent abuse, we keep records of AI requests on our backend. These records may include the request input and prompt, the generated output, the model and provider used, token usage, cost, and timestamps, and they are linked to your account.

When you delete your account, we delete the content of these records: the request input, the prompt, the generated output, and any error details. We keep only the provider, model, status, token usage, cost, and date of each request, without any link to you, so that we can account for our AI costs and usage.

### 3.5 Generated content

Study materials generated for your notes are stored with your notes and synchronized across your devices like other content.

AI-generated content may be inaccurate or incomplete. Please review it before relying on it.

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy or as required by applicable law.

In general:

## 9. Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your Fluxo account and the data we store on our backend.

### Delete through the Fluxo app

Fluxo provides an account-deletion option in the application's settings.

The deletion flow may require reasonable identity verification or confirmation, for example through a confirmation link sent to your email address, to prevent accidental or unauthorized deletion.

### Delete by contacting us

You may also request deletion by emailing:

**[fluxo.admin@gmail.com](mailto:fluxo.admin@gmail.com)**

Please send the request from the email address associated with your Fluxo account where possible.

### What we delete

Following a valid deletion request, we will delete or anonymize personal information associated with your Fluxo account that we control, unless retention is required or permitted by applicable law.

This includes:

* account and profile information, including your name and profile photo;
* authentication records that are no longer required;
* onboarding answers and settings;
* notes, spaces, folders, and their content;
* images, photos, and files stored for your notes;
* learning statistics, review history, activity, and streaks;
* generated study materials and your AI credit records; and
* other server-side data directly associated with the account.

We also delete the content of your AI request records, keeping only anonymous usage data as described in Section 3.4.

Where appropriate, we will also take reasonable steps to instruct relevant processors to delete data associated with your account, subject to their legal and contractual retention requirements.

**Account deletion is permanent.** Once your synced content has been deleted from our backend, we cannot restore it.

### Content on your devices

Deleting your account removes the server-side copy of your content. When you delete your account in the application, the copy stored on that device is erased as well. A deletion request by email or through our website cannot reach your devices: uninstall Fluxo or clear its data to remove copies stored on them.

You can also request deletion without the app on our [account deletion page](/delete-account/).

### Subscriptions after account deletion

Deleting your Fluxo account or uninstalling Fluxo does **not necessarily cancel an active subscription** processed by Apple App Store, Google Play, or another billing provider.

You are responsible for managing or cancelling a platform subscription through the applicable billing provider unless Fluxo expressly confirms otherwise.

Billing providers may retain transaction records where required by law.
