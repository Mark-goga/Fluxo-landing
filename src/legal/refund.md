# Refund Policy

**Last updated: November 5, 2026**

This Refund Policy explains how refunds work for **Fluxo Boost** subscriptions. Fluxo is operated by **Markiian Halabut** ("Fluxo", "we", "us", or "our").

Who handles a refund depends on where you bought your subscription.

## 1. Purchases through the Apple App Store

Subscriptions bought in the iPhone or iPad app are sold and billed by Apple.

Only Apple can issue a refund for an App Store purchase. Request it at https://reportaproblem.apple.com or through Apple Support. Apple's refund rules and applicable law decide the outcome.

## 2. Purchases through Google Play

Subscriptions bought in the Android app are sold and billed through Google Play.

Request a refund through Google Play (https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637). Google's refund rules and applicable law decide the outcome. Depending on the timing of the request, Google may refer you to us; in that case, contact us using the details below.

## 3. Purchases on the Fluxo website

Subscriptions bought on the Fluxo website are sold by our online reseller **Paddle.com**. Paddle is the Merchant of Record for these orders: it processes the payment, issues the receipt, and handles refunds.

These purchases are subject to the [Paddle Buyer Terms](https://www.paddle.com/legal/checkout-buyer-terms) and the [Paddle Refund Policy](https://www.paddle.com/legal/refund-policy).

To request a refund for a website purchase:

* use the link in the receipt email from Paddle, or visit https://paddle.net to find your order; or
* contact us at **[fluxo.admin@gmail.com](mailto:fluxo.admin@gmail.com)** with the email address you used at checkout, and we will pass your request to Paddle.

## 4. Your statutory rights

Nothing in this policy limits rights you have under mandatory consumer law.

If you live in the European Union, the European Economic Area, Switzerland, or the United Kingdom, you may have a 14-day right to withdraw from a digital subscription and receive a full refund, as described in the Paddle Refund Policy for website purchases and in Apple's and Google's terms for store purchases. Where a subscription starts with a free trial, the withdrawal period for the first payment is counted from the end of the trial, as described in the Paddle Refund Policy.

## 5. Charged by mistake or something does not work

If you were charged in error, or Fluxo Boost did not work as described, contact us at **[fluxo.admin@gmail.com](mailto:fluxo.admin@gmail.com)**. We review every request in good faith and help you reach the right party. For App Store and Google Play purchases, the final decision belongs to Apple or Google.

## 6. Free trials

Fluxo Boost may start with a free trial. If you do not want to be charged, cancel before the trial ends:

* **App Store:** Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone or iPad.
* **Google Play:** Google Play → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
* **Website:** the link in the receipt email from Paddle, or https://paddle.net.

## 7. Cancelling is not a refund

Cancelling stops future renewals. You keep Fluxo Boost until the end of the period you have already paid for, and no refund is issued for that period unless a refund right above applies.

Deleting your Fluxo account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it through the place where you bought it.

## 8. After a refund

When a refund is issued, access to Fluxo Boost for the refunded period ends and your account returns to the free plan.
